# SDG Method Phase 5: Interpretation of Results and Recommendations

## Overview

**Phase 5** is the final phase of the SDG assessment methodology. It synthesizes all findings from Phases 1-4 into actionable insights and stakeholder recommendations. Think of this phase as preparing an **elevator pitch** - concise, impactful communication of your sustainability assessment.

---

## The Three Core Deliverables of Phase 5

### 1. Identify Most Important SDG Impacts (150-200 words)

Describe the **three most important positive** and **three most important negative** SDG impacts caused by the technological change.

**Key questions to answer:**
- Which are the **most important sustainability aspects**?
- Which SDGs are impacted by the project?
- In which **life cycle stages** do they appear?
- **What causes them**?

---

### 2. Improvement Possibilities and Trade-offs (150-200 words)

Analyze how negative impacts can be reduced and discuss SDG trade-offs.

**Key questions to answer:**
- Which **interrelations** do you see **between the SDGs**?
- Do you see **consequences between life cycle stages**?
- **Where** in the life cycle can you introduce change to:
  - Reduce negative impacts
  - Increase positive impacts
- **Which specific changes** could that be?

**Trade-off Definition:** When improving impact on one SDG makes another SDG impact worse.

---

### 3. Stakeholder Recommendations (150-200 words)

Provide **specific recommendations** for case-relevant stakeholders.

**Key questions to answer:**
- Which **short-term** recommendations?
- Which **long-term** recommendations?
- To **which decision makers** around the case?

**Stakeholder categories:**
- Politicians / Policymakers
- Companies / Industry
- Users / Consumers
- Researchers / Academia
- NGOs / Civil society

## Worked Example: ICE to EV Technology Shift

The lecture uses the transition from **Internal Combustion Engines (ICEs) to Electric Vehicles (EVs)** to illustrate Phase 5 analysis.

### Baseline vs. New System Comparison

| System | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Baseline** | Slower transition; ICEs remain dominant longer |
| **New System** | Accelerated EV adoption; battery EVs become majority by 2030 |

### Tracing Impacts Across Life Cycle Stages

#### Positive Impacts (Use Phase)
- **"Decreased consumption of fossil and bioenergy fuels in ICEs"**
  - Reduces emissions during vehicle operation
  - Benefits: SDG 13 (Climate Action), SDG 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy)

#### Positive Impacts (Raw Materials Phase)
- **"Decreased extraction of fossil fuels for production of transport fuels for ICEs"**
  - Less oil/gas extraction needed
  - Benefits: SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption), SDG 15 (Life on Land)

### Negative Impacts (Introduced by EVs)

#### Use Phase
- **"Increased electricity consumption in EVs"**
  - Creates new energy demand
  - Impact depends on electricity grid mix

#### Raw Materials Phase
- **"Increased extraction of fossil fuels and production of bioenergy to generate electricity for charging EVs"**
  - Shifts burden from direct fuel to electricity generation
  - Trade-off with reduced tailpipe emissions

- **"Increased extraction of rare earth elements needed for production of electric drive motors of EVs"**
  - New resource extraction requirements
  - Impacts: SDG 15 (Life on Land), SDG 8 (Decent Work), SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption)

#### Production Phase
- **"Increased production of EV batteries and supporting systems"**
  - Manufacturing impacts
  - Energy-intensive processes
  - Supply chain considerations

#### End-of-Life Phase
- **"Increased waste management of EV batteries"**
  - New waste stream
  - Recycling challenges
  - Opportunity for circular economy

## SDG Trade-offs Framework

### Understanding Trade-offs

A **trade-off** occurs when an action that improves one SDG simultaneously worsens another.

### Example Trade-offs in EV Transition

| Positive Effect | Negative Effect (Trade-off) |
|-----------------|----------------------------|
| Reduced fossil fuel combustion (SDG 13) | Increased rare earth mining impacts (SDG 15) |
| Cleaner urban air (SDG 3, SDG 11) | Battery production emissions (SDG 13) |
| Reduced oil dependence (SDG 7) | Increased electricity demand (SDG 7) |

### Analyzing Trade-offs

1. **Identify linked SDGs** - Which goals move in opposite directions?
2. **Trace to life cycle stage** - Where does the trade-off originate?
3. **Assess magnitude** - Is the trade-off acceptable given net benefits?
4. **Propose mitigations** - Can the negative side be reduced?

## Crafting Effective Recommendations

### Characteristics of Good Recommendations

- **Specific** - Targeted to particular stakeholders and actions
- **Actionable** - Clear steps that can be implemented
- **Time-bound** - Distinguish short-term vs. long-term
- **Evidence-based** - Grounded in your Phase 1-4 analysis

### Stakeholder-Specific Approach

| Stakeholder | Focus Areas |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Politicians** | Policy instruments, regulations, incentives |
| **Companies** | Business practices, supply chain, innovation |
| **Users** | Behavior change, purchasing decisions |
| **Researchers** | Knowledge gaps, technology development |

### The Elevator Pitch Mindset

Phase 5 recommendations should be deliverable as an **elevator pitch**:
- Concise and memorable
- Focus on key findings
- Actionable takeaways
- Tailored to audience

## Phase 5 Checklist for Exam Preparation

### Required Outputs

- [ ] Three most important **positive** SDG impacts identified and described
- [ ] Three most important **negative** SDG impacts identified and described
- [ ] Improvement possibilities for negative impacts
- [ ] Trade-offs between SDGs discussed
- [ ] Specific recommendations for relevant stakeholders
- [ ] Short-term and long-term recommendations distinguished

### Key Analytical Questions

1. Which SDGs are impacted and in which life cycle stages?
2. What causes these impacts?
3. What interrelations exist between SDGs?
4. What are the consequences between life cycle stages?
5. Where can change be introduced to improve outcomes?
6. Which stakeholders should receive which recommendations?

- **Effect impacts**: Scored SDG impacts from Phase 4 (Pos/Neg, Likelihood, Size)
- **Stakeholder recommendations**: Targeted, actionable guidance for decision makers
