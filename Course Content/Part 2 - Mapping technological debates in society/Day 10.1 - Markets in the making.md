# Day 10.1 - Markets in the making

---

The purpose of this session is to reflect on the question: ‘What is a market?’. You are introduced to Michel Callon’s market theory that advances the perspective that markets are not natural or pre-existing but are actively constructed through socio-technical processes. This theory emphasises the role of ‘market devices’ (e.g., models, equations, tools) that shape markets and broader socio-technical agencements (STAs) in which humans and non-humans collaborate to facilitate market activities.
