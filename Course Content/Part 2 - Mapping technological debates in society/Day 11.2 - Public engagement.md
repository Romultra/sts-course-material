# Day 11.2 - Public engagement

---

Technological projects frequently meet resistance from the public. This could be due to the fact that stakeholders lack literacy about the technology in question, but the same stakeholders could also have legitimate concerns, that are not properly addressed by the project. How do you know, if the latter is the case? And, if it is, how do you meaningfully take such concerns into account? It is only clear who the stakeholders are and what they are concerned about once the project is mature enough for its implications to become evident, but at this stage many decisions have already been locked-in and are hard to change. To solve this problem we need to decide how, when and why to engage who in technology development.
