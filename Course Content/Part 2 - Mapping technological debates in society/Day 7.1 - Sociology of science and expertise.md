# Introduction

This lecture introduces the sociology of science and expertise, which examines science as a social practice rather than a purely objective method. It presents key concepts—paradigms, interactional expertise, and trading zones—to explain how scientists manage uncertainty, why experts may disagree, and how interdisciplinary communication works. More details are provided in the attached lecture note and the further readings listed below.





# Further readings
